**Description**

Know your real profit on every menu item, every event and every month.

Food Truck Profit & Pricing System PRO is a complete financial toolkit for food trucks, trailers, street-food stalls and small caterers. You type your ingredients, recipes, prices, sales and costs into clearly marked cells — the workbook does the maths and shows you what to fix.

★ WHAT IT DOES
• Executive Dashboard — revenue, gross profit, net profit, margins, monthly operating expenses, waste, break-even progress, monthly chart, best sellers, most profitable items and alerts
• Smart Menu Pricing — full cost per serving (ingredients, packaging, prep labour, other costs), suggested price at your target margin, actual margin AND markup (calculated separately and correctly), food cost %, and a clear status: LOSS · Below target · High food cost · Healthy
• Recipe Builder with unit conversion — buy in lb, kg, gallons or cases; use in oz, g, ml, tbsp or cups. Incompatible units (e.g. "each" vs "grams") are flagged, never silently costed
• Inventory & Waste — stock falls automatically as you sell; reorder alerts; stock value; waste valued in money
• Sales & Expense tracker — 3,000 sales rows and 1,500 expense rows with automatic revenue, cost and profit; 12-month profit & loss report
• Break-Even Calculator — items and revenue you need each month (and per trading day), target-profit planning, margin of safety, chart
• Event Profit Predictor — worst / base / best case, break-even orders and attendance, risk verdict, then actual results vs forecast
• Scenario Simulator — test ingredient inflation, price rises, busy or slow seasons, wage and pitch-fee increases without changing your real data; shows the price you'd need to keep today's profit

★ BUILT TO BE TRUSTED
• One clear profit model: Revenue − cost of goods sold = gross profit; minus waste and operating expenses = net profit
• Nothing counted twice: labour, ingredient purchases and owner's draw are handled so they never double up
• Forecasts are kept separate from actual results
• Error-checked rows: bad dates, unknown items or missing prices are flagged and not counted

★ FOR US & UK SELLERS
Switch between USD ($) and GBP (£) in one click. Symbols change; your amounts are never converted with a made-up exchange rate. US and UK fluid ounces, pints and gallons included.

★ WHAT'S INCLUDED (instant download, .zip)

★ REQUIREMENTS
Microsoft Excel 2010 or later (Windows or Mac) or a free Google account for Google Sheets. No macros, add-ins or subscriptions. A computer is recommended (mobile apps work for viewing).
